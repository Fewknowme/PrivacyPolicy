# ArthZen Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 1, 2026**

**Last updated: August 1, 2026**

RMDevelopers ("we," "us," or "our") provides the ArthZen Android application
("ArthZen" or the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use
ArthZen.

ArthZen is a private, local-first financial ledger. It does not connect to bank accounts, read
messages or notifications, or upload the financial records you enter to RMDevelopers.

## 1. Information stored locally on your device

ArthZen stores the information you choose to enter in the App's local database, including:

- Your display name and App preferences.
- Accounts, income and expense entries, categories, notes, and dates.
- Budget settings, savings plans, contributions, and locally calculated summaries.
- Local settings needed to operate the App, including Premium entitlement and review-access state.

This information remains on your device. RMDevelopers does not operate a server that receives or
stores your ArthZen profile, ledger, budgets, plans, notes, or calculations.

ArthZen stores monetary amounts as part of your local ledger, but it does not collect payment-card
details, bank login credentials, or bank-account data.

## 2. Advertising and data processed by Google

The free version of ArthZen displays a Google Mobile Ads banner after applicable consent
requirements are resolved. ArthZen requests non-personalized ads. Premium users are not served ads
by the App.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following information with
Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention, security, and compliance purposes:

- Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
- App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad interactions.
- Diagnostic information about App and SDK performance.
- Device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID or app set ID when available.

This information is encrypted in transit. ArthZen does not include your display name, transactions,
accounts, categories, notes, budgets, savings plans, or calculated insights in its ad requests.

Google's handling of this information is governed by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google also publishes
[Google Mobile Ads SDK data-disclosure information](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

## 3. Advertising choices

Where required, ArthZen presents Google's consent flow before requesting ads. If Google reports
that ads cannot be requested, the App continues to work without loading an ad.

When available for your region, you can reopen advertising privacy choices from within ArthZen.
You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through your Android device settings. Purchasing
ArthZen Premium removes advertising requests from the App.

## 4. Premium subscriptions and Google Play

Optional ArthZen Premium subscriptions are offered through Google Play. Google Play processes
payments and may provide the App with purchase, product, entitlement, renewal, and transaction
information needed to activate or restore Premium features. RMDevelopers does not receive or store
your complete payment-card details.

Google Play's handling of purchase and account information is governed by Google's applicable
privacy policy and terms. You can manage or cancel a subscription through Google Play.

## 5. Exports, imports, and sharing

ArthZen lets you explicitly export transaction data or a local restore backup and import a backup.
These actions use Android's system file picker or share interface. ArthZen does not automatically
upload exported files to RMDevelopers.

You choose the destination and are responsible for files after they leave the App's private
storage. Copies saved to another app, cloud provider, email service, or device are governed by that
recipient's privacy practices and are not deleted when you reset ArthZen.

## 6. Device authentication

If you enable device authentication for App access, Android performs the authentication using its
system security services. ArthZen does not receive or store your fingerprint, face template, device
passcode, or other biometric data.

## 7. Data retention, deletion, and backups

Your locally stored ArthZen information remains on your device until you edit or delete individual
records, use **Reset app**, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.

- You can edit or delete user-created records within the App.
- **Reset app** removes the local profile, ledger, accounts, plans, categories, and settings.
- Android operating-system backup is disabled for ArthZen App data.
- RMDevelopers cannot recover deleted local data because it does not have a server copy.
- Files you exported must be deleted separately from their saved locations.

ArthZen does not provide developer-side account deletion because it does not create an ArthZen
account or store your ledger on RMDevelopers servers.

## 8. Security

ArthZen keeps your financial records in the App's private local storage and disables Android App
backup. Network data used by Google Mobile Ads and Google Play is encrypted in transit by those
services. You should protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android and ArthZen updated.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security
for information stored on your device or processed by third-party services.

## 9. Children's privacy

ArthZen is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not
knowingly collect children's financial information. If you believe a child has provided
information to us, contact us using the address below.

## 10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ArthZen's features, service providers, or legal obligations
change. We will publish the updated policy at this location and revise the "Last updated" date.
