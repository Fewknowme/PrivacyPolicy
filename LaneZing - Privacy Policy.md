# LaneZing: Highway Bike Racer

Privacy policy · RMDevelopers
Effective [RELEASE DATE]



## Your game stays on your device

LaneZing is an offline motorbike riding game. It has no account, login, game server, cloud save, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service, and no in-app purchases. The game does not collect, send or share any personal data, and nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

Progress is saved locally on your device only. This includes career progress, coins, owned bikes, paints, stickers and gloves, best scores and route times, daily challenge progress and settings such as volume, haptics, steering, controls, graphics, units, traffic and weather choices. We do not send this saved game content to our own server or to anyone else.



## Permissions and links

The game uses vibration for optional haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

The art credits page contains links to the websites of the creators of some 3D models and to the Creative Commons licence. These open in your browser only when you tap them; those websites have their own privacy policies.



## Your choices and deletion

You may play fully offline. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove all local progress and settings. There is no RMDevelopers player account to delete or cloud backup to recover.



## Audience and support

This general-audience racing game is intended for teenagers and adults, not specifically directed to children under 13. It does not ask players for their age, name or contact details. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your request; do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.


For privacy or support requests, contact RMDevelopers at [rohitmadasvpn@gmail.com](mailto:rohitmadasvpn@gmail.com). Include “LaneZing” in the subject. This is also our contact for concerns about children's data.



## Policy changes

We update this page when game data practices change and revise the effective date. If a future version adds features such as advertising, purchases or crash reporting, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Material changes will also be disclosed in the app or store listing as appropriate.
