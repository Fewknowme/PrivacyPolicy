# LaneZing: Highway Bike Racer

Privacy policy · RMDevelopers
Effective 14 October 2026



## Your game progress

LaneZing is a motorbike riding game for Android and iOS. It has no RMDevelopers account, login or game server. Career progress, coins, owned bikes, paints, stickers and gloves, best scores and route times, daily challenge progress, ad reward counts and settings (such as volume, haptics, steering, controls, graphics, units, traffic and weather) are stored locally on your device. We do not send this saved game content to our own server.

You can choose a rider name (up to 14 characters) shown on race grids and results. It is optional, you can change it any time in Settings, and it can be any nickname; we suggest not using your real full name. It is stored in your career save on the device and, if you use cloud save, in your Google Play Games saved game. It is not shown to other players, not shared with anyone and not sent to an RMDevelopers server, and it is deleted together with the save.



## Cloud save with Google Play Games (Android)

On Android, LaneZing can back up your career to your own Google account through Google Play Games Services "Saved Games", so a new or reset phone gets its progress back. This is optional: the game is fully playable without it.

- **Sign-in.** If you already use Google Play Games on your device, Play Games may sign you in automatically when the game starts; otherwise you can sign in from Settings → Cloud Save. Google handles sign-in; we never see your Google password or email address.
- **What is saved.** One save slot holding your career save (your rider name, coins and total coins earned, owned bikes, upgrades, paints, stickers and gloves, completed routes and goals, daily challenge and rewarded-ad counts, distance ridden) and your two best scores, plus a short description (your coin balance) and a progress number Google uses to pick the copy with more progress. Settings stay on the device. No screenshots, contacts or location are saved.
- **Where it lives.** The save is stored by Google in your Google account, not on an RMDevelopers server, and only this game can read or write it. Google Play Games also processes your Play Games player ID and profile (gamer name and avatar) and collects diagnostics and usage information about its SDK, as described in [Google's Play Games Services data disclosure](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection). Data is encrypted in transit.
- **When it syncs.** At launch, after your career changes (at most about once a minute, never during a ride), every few minutes while playing, and when you leave the app.

Cloud save is not available on iOS; progress there stays on the device.



## Online advertising

The app includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). When network access and your privacy choices permit, Google's advertising SDK may collect and share:

- device and advertising identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, on iOS, the IDFA if you allow tracking
- IP address, which can indicate approximate location
- device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version
- ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped
- diagnostic and performance information from the advertising SDK

This information is used to show and measure ads, for analytics about ad performance and to prevent fraud. Google's SDK documentation states that this information is encrypted in transit. The game does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo permissions, and RMDevelopers does not receive your advertising ID or link it to you.

LaneZing shows two kinds of ads. Most are optional rewarded videos: you choose to watch one, for example to double a ride's or race's coins or earn bonus coins, and the reward is given only after the video finishes. Rewarded ads never start on their own. The game also shows an occasional skippable full-screen ad when you return to the garage after a ride: never during a ride or race, never the first time you play, and at most once every few rides and several minutes. Both kinds come from Google AdMob under the consent choices described here. The game can be played without an internet connection; ads are simply unavailable offline.

See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), [Google's advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and the [Google Mobile Ads data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure) for Google's practices.



## Consent and your choices

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, a Google consent message appears before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy options button in the game's Settings. If you do not consent to personalised advertising, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads where lawful; this does not restrict gameplay.

On iOS, the game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads on some devices).



## Deletion

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local progress and settings. There is no RMDevelopers player account to delete. If you used cloud save, your saved game stays in your Google account after uninstalling; delete it, or your whole Play Games profile, in Google Play Games (your Play Games profile settings, which can delete one game's data or the whole Play Games account) or from your Google Account's data controls. Deleting it there also stops the game restoring that progress. Google's retention and deletion practices apply to data processed by its advertising services; use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for those requests.



## Audience and children's privacy

This general-audience racing game is intended for teenagers and adults and is not directed to children under 13. It does not ask players for their age or contact details (the optional rider name can be any nickname), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ad requests are not marked as child-directed, and ads are limited to content suitable for teens. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your request; do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.
