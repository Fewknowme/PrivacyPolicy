# Patang Pop: Kite Puzzle

Privacy policy · RMDevelopers
Effective 13 September 2026



## Your game stays on your device

Patang Pop is an offline puzzle game. It has no account, game server, cloud save, purchases or subscriptions. Campaign levels and artwork are included in the app; random puzzles are generated on your device. Progress, best stars, active playing time, per-level best completion times, cosmetic thread, hint credits and reward-claim tokens, random puzzle saves and cleared counts, unlocked colours, the last daily-gift claim date and settings are stored locally. We do not send this saved game content to our own server.



## Optional local reminders

If you turn on play reminders and allow Android notifications, the app schedules quiet puzzle reminders on your device. Reminder preferences, recent activity time, notification time and reminder count stay locally. No Firebase integration, push token, remote messaging server or reminder analytics service is used. Reminders work offline and stop after three until you return. You can switch them off in the game Settings or Android notification settings. Android battery restrictions may delay delivery.

## Online advertising

The Android app includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. When network access and applicable privacy choices permit, Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and account identifiers including advertising ID and app set ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. These are used for advertising, measurement/analytics, and fraud prevention. Google's SDK documentation states that this information is encrypted in transit. The game does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

Campaign and random puzzles, saved hint credits, retries, level rewards and cosmetic unlocks are available without an internet connection. Players start with three hint credits; replenishing hints requires a completed rewarded video, with a maximum of three saved credits. When a retry ad is available online, completing it starts a fresh attempt; closing early keeps the current attempt. Offline or unavailable retry ads allow a free retry. Optional rewarded videos provide extra cosmetic thread after completion, including a daily gift that can be claimed once per device calendar day. No account or login is required for this gift. Occasional interstitial ads may appear at campaign level transitions when online. Bottom banner ads may appear on all game screens, including during play, when an ad is available. Banners sit separately below the game controls. Full-screen ads do not interrupt active puzzle moves.

See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Google's advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and [Google Mobile Ads data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure) for Google's practices.



## Your choices and deletion

Where required, a Google consent message appears. Applicable ad privacy choices are available from Settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID using Android's privacy/ad settings. Denying personalised advertising may still permit limited or contextual ads where lawful; it does not restrict gameplay. You may play offline. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local progress and settings. There is no RMDevelopers player account to delete or cloud backup to recover. Google's retention and deletion practices apply to data processed by its services; use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for those requests.



## Audience and support

This general-audience logic game is intended for teenagers and adults, not specifically directed to children under 13. It does not ask players for their age, name or contact details. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your request; do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.
